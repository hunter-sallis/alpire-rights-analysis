Reading the categories

Represented (E) is a positive result: your interest is represented on the record carrying the recording and nothing else is outstanding. It does not mean every other represented song is perfect — a song can be represented and have a linkage or duplicate problem, in which case it sits under that problem with its representation shown in the song record. Representation gap (A) is no work, or a work with no publisher representing you — no publisher naming you and no administrator of your interest, including records with no publisher claim at all. Duplicate / conflict (B) and recording linkage (C) are data-relationship problems. Whether your writer name appears on a record is shown as a dimension in every song record; on a represented record it is a metadata note, not an issue.
How to read this report. Findings describe the condition of the public registration record on the report date across four independent questions: representation, attribution, linkage and work status. None states that money is owed, unpaid or lost, and none infers ownership percentages. Plays are shown to indicate where exposure is concentrated.
